About the Photographer

Hi, I'm Susi.

A photographer drawn to honest light and the quiet between the big moments.

Storytelling through stillness.

I'm an engagement, event, and grad photographer based in Dallas, Texas, with a love for soft daylight, slow afternoons, and people who lean into the camera the way they would a close friend. My work lives somewhere between documentary and editorial, careful enough to feel intentional, loose enough to let real moments through.

Whether I'm with a couple on a slow engagement afternoon, with a graduate stepping out of the chapter they just finished, or weaving through the warm crowd of a celebration, my goal is the same: photographs that feel like you, that hold up a decade from now, and that you actually want to print.

A Few Things I Love

Overcast mornings · warm kitchen light · linen and wool · second-hand bookshops · sparkling water on long drives · the half-second after the laugh.

Approach

I shoot a thoughtful mix of film and digital, lean into natural light, and keep direction gentle. You don't need to be "good in front of the camera." That's my job. Come as you are.
