Privacy

Your information, plainly.

What I collect when you inquire or view a gallery, and what I do with it.

Last updated November 16, 2026

Collective by Sus is a one-person photography business run by Susi in Dallas, Texas. This page explains what information I collect through this website, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Email contact@collectivebysus.com.

What I collect

  • When you send an inquiry: your name and email, and anything else you choose to share: phone number, Instagram handle, session type, the days you're open to shooting, location ideas, number of people, how you plan to use the photos, how you found me, and your message.
  • When you view a private gallery: the photos from your session, the photos you mark as favorites, and any note you send with your selections.
  • When you pay: I keep a record of the amount, date, and how you paid (for example Venmo or Zelle). I never see or store card numbers.
  • To keep the site safe: when you send a form, your IP address is scrambled (hashed) and used only to stop spam. It's never stored in readable form and is cleared after about a day. The site may also use Cloudflare's spam check, which runs in your browser.

How I use it

  • To reply to you, plan and schedule your session, and deliver your photos.
  • To keep basic business records, like what was booked and what was paid.

I don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. If your gallery has a PIN, one cookie remembers that you entered it, so you don't have to type it again. It lasts up to 60 days. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, may set cookies it needs for security.

Who else handles it

A few services help run this site. They only get what they need to do their job:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, stores gallery photos, and protects the site from spam and attacks.
  • An email provider delivers the replies and notifications I send you.

I'll also share information if the law requires it.

Your photos

Galleries are private. Only people with the link (and PIN, if there is one) can see them. I only post photos from your session on my portfolio or social media with your permission. You can change your mind at any time, and I'll take them down.

Clients under 18

For graduation and other sessions with clients under 18, I work with a parent or guardian and may ask for their contact information. A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct, or delete their child's information at any time.

How long I keep it

I keep client records as long as I need them for your session and for my business and tax records. Galleries stay online until they expire or I remove them. Inquiries that don't turn into a booking are deleted when I no longer need them. You can always ask me to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Just email contact@collectivebysus.com. I'll respond within 30 days. If I need to keep something for tax records, I'll tell you.

Keeping it safe

The admin area is protected by a sign-in that only I can pass. Gallery links are long and random, and can have a PIN. No system is perfect, but I keep what I collect to what I actually need.

Changes

If this page changes, I'll update the date at the top.
