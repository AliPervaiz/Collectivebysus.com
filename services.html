Investment & Offerings

Services

Sessions are built around your day. These starting points give a sense of what's included.

01 · Couples

Engagements

A slow afternoon for the two of you, the kind that feels like a date you happen to have a camera at. Lovely for save-the-dates, pre-wedding sessions, anniversaries, or just because you said yes.

  • 60–90 minute session
  • One or two locations
  • Outfit changes encouraged
  • 40+ hand-edited high-resolution images
  • Save-the-date design guidance

Starting at $1,000

02 · Gatherings

Events

Birthdays, showers, anniversaries, milestone parties: anything worth remembering. Light, candid coverage with a few intentional group shots so you end up with both the feel of the night and the keepsakes from it.

  • 2–4 hour event coverage
  • Candid documentary + light group portraits
  • Private online gallery, hand-edited
  • 50+ high-resolution images
  • Travel within DFW included

Starting at $275

03 · Milestones

Grads

A relaxed celebration of the chapter you just finished. On campus, in a favorite neighborhood, or somewhere meaningful to you. Bring your gown, your friends, your dog.

  • 60–90 minute session
  • One or two locations
  • 2–3 outfit changes
  • 40+ hand-edited high-resolution images
  • Solo, duo, and small-group options

Starting at $125
